PUBLIC TOOL / AGENTIC DISCOVERY

Is your website agentic-ready?

Run a public browser-based readiness scan for signals that help search engines and AI systems discover, interpret and retrieve a website: crawlability, canonical URLs, semantic HTML, structured data, robots policy, sitemap, llms.txt and machine-readable service information.

This is a heuristic readiness check, not a certification and not a guarantee of inclusion in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity or another system.

What is being checked?

The scanner reports publicly observable technical signals. There is no universal “agentic enabled” switch for websites. Readiness depends on crawlability, clear content, machine-readable entities, structured data and each AI/search system's own ingestion policies.

  • HTTPS and reachable homepage
  • Title, description, language and canonical
  • robots.txt and XML sitemap
  • Optional llms.txt
  • JSON-LD and recognizable entity types
  • Semantic headings and crawlable text
  • Open Graph metadata
  • AI/search crawler directives in robots.txt
  • Internal links and service/technology terminology

Google documents structured data as a way to help it understand page content. Google also clarified in June 2026 that llms.txt is not required for Google Search and does not itself increase or decrease Google visibility.
