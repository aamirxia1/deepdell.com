PUBLIC TOOL / AGENTIC DISCOVERY

Is your website agentic-ready?

Run a public, browser-based readiness scan for the signals that help search engines and AI systems discover, interpret and retrieve a website: crawlability, canonical URLs, semantic HTML, structured data, robots policy, sitemap, llms.txt and machine-readable service information.

This is a heuristic readiness check, not a certification or a guarantee of inclusion in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity or any other system.

What the scanner checks

The scanner evaluates publicly observable technical signals rather than claiming that a site is “enabled” for a particular AI. It checks the things a browser can reasonably verify and reports gaps separately.

  • HTTPS and reachable homepage
  • Title, description, language and canonical URL
  • Robots.txt and sitemap availability
  • llms.txt availability as an optional supplemental signal
  • JSON-LD structured data and entity types
  • Semantic headings and crawlable page text
  • Open Graph and social metadata
  • Explicit AI/search crawler directives when present in robots.txt
  • Internal navigation and machine-readable service/entity signals
